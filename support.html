Restore purchases

If you already subscribed or bought Lifetime Access, open the paywall or go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account used for the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Visit account.microsoft.com/services, sign in, and choose GCSE English Revision under Services & subscriptions.

Papers and skills

Practice covers GCSE English Language (reading, language and structure analysis, creative and transactional writing, SPaG) and English Literature (poetry, prose, drama, character, theme and context). You can switch between Language and Literature at any time in Settings. Timed mock papers use 40 questions in 45 minutes with a pass mark of 28/40 (70%).

Keyboard shortcuts

In a quiz: A–D or 1–4 to answer, Enter for next, ← for the previous question, Esc to leave. In flashcards: Space to flip, 1 review again, 2 got it.
