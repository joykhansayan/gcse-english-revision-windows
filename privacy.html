Privacy Policy

GCSE English Revision for Windows · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GCSE English Revision for Windows (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no analytics and no advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

Your study progress, quiz and mock results, bookmarks, flashcard schedule, streak and preferences (Language or Literature mode, daily goal, optional exam date, reminder preference) are stored only in the App's local storage on your Windows device. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or reset progress in Settings.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. We never see or receive payment card details. Microsoft's handling of purchase data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Network use

The App works offline. It connects to the internet only to download app updates, to let the Microsoft Store verify your purchases, and when you choose to open an external link (such as an exam board's GCSE English page) in your browser.

5. No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

6. Children

The App is a revision tool for students preparing for GCSE English (typically aged 14–16) and for adults resitting the exam. Because the App collects no personal data at all, it does not collect personal data from children. Purchases are made through the Microsoft Store, where Microsoft family settings let parents approve or block purchases.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact
